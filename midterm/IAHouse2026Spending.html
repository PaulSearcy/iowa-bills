How the totals are built

Candidate committee spending is every expenditure the nominee's committee reported since Nov 6, 2024 (loan repayments excluded). In-kind is goods and services given to the campaign, mostly mail, staff and ads paid for by the state parties; the Republican Party of Iowa gave about $3.81M in-kind to House nominees vs. about $1.15M from the Iowa Democratic Party. Outside groups are independent expenditures by groups not registered in Iowa, counted for a candidate when they support that candidate or attack the opponent. Spending by Iowa-registered PACs that is not reported as an in-kind gift to a specific candidate is not attributed to a district.

Kevin W. DuBay (R, HD 31) has no registered committee (one is only required after raising or spending more than $1,000), and Charlie Helmick (R, HD 97) has a committee that reported no spending, so both show $0. Totals stop around Oct 14; the final weeks of the campaign appear in later filings.

How the results are measured

The winning margin is the winner's votes minus the runner-up's votes. Points are that margin as a share of all votes cast in the race, write-ins included. A candidate who lost shows how far behind the winner they finished. In races with only one candidate on the ballot, the margin is measured against write-ins and the race is labeled unopposed.

Berleen Wobeter (D, HD 53) and Bruce A. Michalowski (R, HD 36) were on the general-election ballot but were not in the candidate list the money data was built from, so their spending is shown as "not tracked" and those two districts are left out of the money comparisons.
