How the totals are built

Candidate committee spending is every expenditure the nominee's committee reported since Nov 6, 2024 (loan repayments excluded). In-kind is goods and services given to the campaign, mostly mail, staff and ads paid for by the state parties; the Republican Party of Iowa gave about $2.38M in-kind to Senate nominees (plus about $103K from its Senate Majority Fund) vs. about $1.14M from the Iowa Democratic Party. Outside groups are independent expenditures by groups not registered in Iowa, counted for a candidate when they support that candidate or attack the opponent. Spending by Iowa-registered PACs that is not reported as an in-kind gift to a specific candidate is not attributed to a district.

Senators not up for election in 2026 (the even-numbered districts) are not shown, even if their committees spent money this cycle. Totals stop around Oct 14; the final weeks of the campaign appear in later filings.
