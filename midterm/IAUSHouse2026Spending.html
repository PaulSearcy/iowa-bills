How the totals are built

Candidate spending is total disbursements by the candidate's principal campaign committee for the full 2026 cycle, primary and general combined. Outside money is independent expenditures reported on FEC Schedule E: spending that supports a candidate or opposes their opponent counts for that candidate's side (pro-D is support for the Democrat plus opposition to the Republican; pro-R is the reverse). Coordinated spending by the national and state parties is not included, nor is spending by groups that is not reported as an independent expenditure (such as issue ads).

Candidate totals stop at the pre-general reports covering through Oct 14. The last three weeks of the race appear in the post-general reports, due Dec 3, 2026. Some report dates for candidates who left the race reflect their last filing, which can be a termination or year-end report.

How the results are measured

The winning margin is the winner's votes minus the runner-up's votes. Points are that margin as a share of all votes cast in the race, write-ins included. Every other candidate on the ballot shows how far behind the winner they finished. "Outspent" marks a winner whose side had less total money (candidates + outside groups) than the loser's.

Rick Stewart (Libertarian, IA-02) was on the general-election ballot but is not in the FEC data this page was built from, so he appears only in the district breakdown. All four seats were held by Republicans going into the election.
