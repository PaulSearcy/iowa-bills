## 2. Filing nomination papers (Secretary of State)

State House candidates file with the **Iowa Secretary of State**, not the county auditor.

### 2026 calendar (even-year cycle)

| Milestone | Date (2026) |
| --- | --- |
| **Primary election** | Tuesday, **June 2** |
| **Filing window — state & federal offices** | **Feb 23 – Mar 13, 5:00 p.m.** |
| **General election** | Tuesday, **Nov 3** |
| **Filing window — general (only if not primary winner)** | Feb 23 – Jun 2, 5:00 p.m. |

There is **no filing fee** in Iowa.

### Forms (file **together** — if one piece is missing, the filing can be rejected)

| Form | Purpose | PDF |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Affidavit of Candidacy** | Sworn statement of eligibility | [affidavit.pdf](https://sos.iowa.gov/elections/pdf/candidates/affidavit.pdf) |
| **Nomination Petition (primary)** | Signatures to qualify for the Democratic primary | [nompetprim.pdf](https://sos.iowa.gov/elections/pdf/candidates/nompetprim.pdf) |

**State Representative (primary):** **50** valid signatures from **eligible electors who live in your HD** ([SOS summary](https://sos.iowa.gov/news-resources/state-and-federal-candidates-are-set-june-primaries)).

**Petition tips**

- Use only the Secretary of State’s petition form (8½″ × 11″).  
- Each signer needs **signature, full residential address (street, city — no P.O. box alone), and date**.  
- Sheets must be **stapled or bound** as one petition.  
- Make a **copy** for your records before filing.  
- Filing by mail counts only if the SOS **receives** the packet by the deadline (postmark does not matter).  
- File **early** so staff can flag fixable errors before the deadline.

**Where to send:** Secretary of State, Elections Division, First Floor, Lucas Building, 321 E. 12th St., Des Moines, IA 50319. Confirm current instructions in the [2026 Primary Candidate’s Guide](https://sos.iowa.gov/primary-election).

**District map:** [Iowa Legislature — House district maps (PDFs)](https://www.legis.iowa.gov/legislators/districts/house)

## 3. Campaign finance (IECDB)

Download the **[Statehouse candidate packet](https://ethics.iowa.gov/media/43/download?inline=)** (PDF) for the full reporting calendar.

### Register the committee

| Trigger | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Raise, spend, or incur debts **> $1,000** in a calendar year (including your own money) | File **Statement of Organization (DR-1)** within **10 days** |
| Below $1,000 but want standard “paid for by” attribution | Optional **DR-SFA** via [Web Reporting System](https://webapp.iecdb.iowa.gov) |

- Committee name must include your **last name**.  
- Update the DR-1 within **30 days** of any change (treasurer, bank, address, etc.).

### Treasurer

| Requirement | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Who | **Iowa resident**, age **18+** |
| Can the candidate serve? | **Yes** — you may appoint yourself |
| Optional role | **Campaign chair** (may be listed on DR-1) |
| Duties | Segregate funds, deposit within **7 days**, keep itemized records **5 years**, file disclosure reports |

Every person who collects money for the committee must turn it over to the treasurer within **15 days**.

**Legislative session rule:** During the regular legislative session, **do not accept** contributions from **lobbyists or PACs** if you are a member/candidate for the General Assembly ([IECDB candidate guide](https://ethics.iowa.gov/campaigns/candidate-guide)).

### Campaign bank account

| Requirement | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Separate account? | **Yes**, in the **committee’s name**, unless the **only** contributions are from the candidate personally |
| Where | **Iowa** financial institution |
| Name on account | Must match the committee name on the DR-1 |
| EIN | Many banks require an IRS **EIN** ([apply online](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/apply-for-an-employer-identification-number-ein-online)) — not an IECDB rule, but plan for it |
| Disclose on DR-1 | Bank name, address, and account type |

**Opening the account (practical checklist)**

1. Choose treasurer (self or trusted Iowa resident).  
2. Obtain EIN if the bank requires it.  
3. File DR-1 (or ask IECDB to confirm if the bank wants a filed DR-1 first — **ethicsboard@iowa.gov**).  
4. Open account; order checks/debit card in the **committee name only**.  
5. Never mix personal and committee funds; no corporate/bank/insurance contributions ([prohibited contributors](https://ethics.iowa.gov/campaigns/candidate-guide)).

### 2026 disclosure reports (election year)

| Report due | Covers (typical House race) |
| --- | --- |
| **May 19** | Jan 1 – May 14 |
| **July 19** | May 15 – Jul 14 |
| **Oct 19** (or Wed. after Columbus Day) | Jul 15 – Oct 14 |
| **Jan 19** (following year) | Through Dec 31, 2026 |
| **Friday before primary / general** | Short window before each election if activity after last report |
| **Supplementary** | If you receive **≥ $1,000** after primary or general and before the next scheduled report |

All reports file electronically in the **Web Reporting System**: [webapp.iecdb.iowa.gov](https://webapp.iecdb.iowa.gov).

## 4. Printed materials & yard signs

See **[districtPrintShops.md](districtPrintShops.md)** for the recommended vendor nearest **HD ______**.

**Ordering checklist**

- [ ] Get quotes for brochures, postcards, palm cards, and yard signs together (mail often bundles best).  
- [ ] Ask for a **union bug** if your county party or labor allies expect it ([CWA Iowa printers](https://cwaprintshops.com/printers/cat/d7/iowa/)).  
- [ ] Put **“Paid for by [committee name]”** on printed pieces and digital ads ([attribution rules](https://ethics.iowa.gov/campaigns/candidate-guide)). Yard signs **≤ 32 sq ft** are exempt from attribution under state rules, but many campaigns still include it.  
- [ ] Pay the printer from the **committee account**; treasurer logs the expenditure in WRS.

## 8. Compliance reminders

- No use of **public resources** for campaigning ([§ 68A.505](https://www.legis.iowa.gov/docs/code/2026/68A.html)).  
- Know **sign placement** rules: no state/corporate property without permission; 300-foot polling-place limits on election days ([IECDB sign guidance](https://ethics.iowa.gov/campaigns/candidate-guide)).  
- Keep copies of contracts, invoices, and bank statements **five years**.  
- Questions: **ethicsboard@iowa.gov** or (515) 281-4028 before you gamble on a gray area.
