State House candidates file with the Secretary of State, not the county auditor. No filing fee.

Campaign finance (IECDB)

Download the Statehouse candidate packet (PDF) for the full calendar.

Register when you raise, spend, or incur debts over $1,000 in a calendar year (including your own money): file DR-1 within 10 days in the Web Reporting System. Committee name must include your last name.

Treasurer

WhoIowa resident, age 18+. You may serve as your own treasurer.
DutiesDeposit within 7 days; detailed records 5 years; file disclosure reports.
CollectorsAnyone who takes in money must give it to the treasurer within 15 days.

During the legislative session, do not accept lobbyist or PAC money if you are a General Assembly candidate. IECDB candidate guide

Compliance reminders

  • No public resources for campaigning (§ 68A.505).
  • Yard signs: respect property rights; no polling-place buffer violations on election days.
  • Keep contracts, invoices, and bank statements five years.
  • When unsure, call IECDB before you act: ethicsboard@iowa.gov · (515) 281-4028
